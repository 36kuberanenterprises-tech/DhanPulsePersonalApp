# DhanPulse Personal v2.9.7

SENSEX opts into two independent research systems. System 1 retains the six strategy families. System 2 uses the indicator engine, confirmed higher timeframe trend, futures traded average and existing OI, volatility, depth and expiry context. Either system can qualify while the other waits. Opposite qualified directions remain visible independently and automatic execution is disabled. Agreement is a qualitative label, not a measured win probability.

Each system displays an option research plan. Agreement on the same direction, contract and candle uses one call identity and fixed premium levels. Quote samples in the paper journal are not exchange fills. Entry controls reject price extension above 0.35 ATR in the proposed direction, premium extension above 2 percent and remaining reward to risk below 1.5 after a spread allowance. Premium targets are fixed research rules, not forecasts; brokerage and slippage are not included. Missed plans remain invalid for that setup identity.

The SENSEX screen checks every second while active, without overlapping requests. It uses fresh available streaming underlying prices and a shared FULL quote snapshot lasting at most two seconds. Broker response latency and API pacing can make a complete evaluation take longer. Candle checks retain the selected interval. NIFTY, BANKNIFTY, stock scanner and MCX strategy rules are unchanged. Older APKs retain their existing SENSEX policy.
